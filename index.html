I’m Yudhistira, a Product Designer curious about how everyday products shape human behavior.

Based in Jakarta, I’m drawn to the small decisions inside a product, like a default, a label, or a moment of friction. That curiosity guides my work on monetization and growth: helping products create value for the business and for the people using them.

About me

Right now, I design digital products at Kompas Gramedia, taking ideas from first question to shipped screen.

May 2025 — Present. I work end-to-end when a project calls for it: researching, exploring, crafting the UI, and partnering with engineers until it’s live. When it needs something more focused, like a single flow or a set of assets, I step in there too.
